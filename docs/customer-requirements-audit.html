What must be true before claiming full coverage

  1. Every source row has a record or an explicitly linked requirement in the reusable project template.
  2. Each real project records Included, Not applicable with a reason, or Needs confirming for conditional items; unknown does not mean omitted.
  3. Every included action has a responsible role, completion evidence and a usable date or dependency rule. Source notes remain available.
  4. All inspection prerequisites are represented, including rough-work and insulation approvals before drywall where applicable. Failed checks lead to corrections and another review or inspection.
  5. Application, clearance of holds, permit closure, certificate receipt, final reconciliation and closeout review remain distinct.
  6. Manual actions and agent tools preserve the same requirements, status meanings and reminder settings. An agent cannot manufacture a pass or certificate.
  7. Built and demonstrated coverage is tracked separately from captured requirements. The sample demo may show a slice, while the full customer scope stays visible in the project template.

Locations, Gantt views, AI actions, messaging integrations and agent permissions are additional product decisions from our discussion. They supplement the customer checklist and do not replace it.
